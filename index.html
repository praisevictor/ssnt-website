About SSNT

SmartPrep exists for one reason: the child.

Every teacher we train, every school we set up, every audit we run, every shadow we place — it all comes back to a single question. Is this child moving forward?

We work under real Nigerian conditions, not ideal ones. Large classes. Stretched budgets. Families doing their best with little. That is the floor we build for, and everything we make has to work there.

And we do two things, kept deliberately apart. We build the people — training and certifying practitioners who actually know what to do. And we hold the standard — auditing quality independently, so families and schools get the truth, not a sales pitch. One arm coaches. The other referees. That is how our word stays worth something.

Our SEN Solutions Model

Different products. One child at the centre.

Every SmartPrep solution is about the child — even the ones we sell to a school or a therapist.

SCHOOL

A school subscribes for SEN Readiness — so the child walks into a classroom that can actually hold them.

FAMILY

A family gets the Carryover Solution — so the child hears the same support at home, at school, and in therapy.

SCHOOL / PARENT

A school or parent subscribes for a Therapy Audit — so the therapy the child receives is genuinely working, not just happening.

SHADOW

A shadow is placed — so the child has one trained person beside them who knows precisely what to do.
